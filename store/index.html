The Collection

Design Philosophy

Anti-Disposable Architecture.

MONOLITH / ARCHIVE was founded in opposition to the accelerated lifecycle of modern fast fashion. Every silhouette is drafted through ergonomic load-distribution mapping and fabricated in limited runs in Okayama and Kyoto.

All garments utilize zero-waste pattern-cutting methods where possible, with hardware sourced exclusively from certified industrial suppliers including Fidlock Germany and YKK Fastening Systems.
